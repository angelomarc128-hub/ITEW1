About Us

Welcome to THRIFT BY DUDONGS, your go-to thrift shop for pre-loved garments with style, story, and soul.

We believe great fashion doesn't have to cost the earth, or your whole paycheck. Every piece on our racks is carefully selected, from everyday tees, jeans, and dresses to jackets, vintage finds, and one-of-a-kind statement pieces.

Each item is checked for quality, cleaned, and priced fairly, so you can shop with confidence.

  • Carefully Selected

    Tees, jeans, dresses, jackets and vintage finds.

  • Checked & Cleaned

    Every item is quality checked before it hits the rack.

  • Fairly Priced

    Great style without emptying your wallet.
